# GALEON

### Privacy infrastructure for real-world finance

**Website:** [galeon.finance](https://galeon.finance) | **GitHub:** [github.com/mateodaza/galeon](https://github.com/mateodaza/galeon)

## Problem

On-chain finance is fully transparent. Every payment, payroll, and treasury move is public. Businesses can't operate without exposing competitive intelligence. Existing privacy solutions (Tornado, mixers) lack compliance—making them unusable for legitimate use cases.

## Solution

Galeon combines **stealth addresses** (EIP-5564/6538) with **Privacy Pools** to deliver complete transaction privacy with built-in compliance. Receivers get unique addresses per payment. Senders break on-chain links via ZK proofs. Tax reports generated on demand.

## What's Live (Mantle Mainnet)

- **Ports**: Stealth addresses for receiver privacy
- **Privacy Pool**: ZK-based sender privacy (only verified funds can enter)
- **Account Model**: Unlimited deposits → single proof withdrawal
- **Shipwreck**: Tax-ready compliance reports with on-chain receipts
- **Client-side proofs**: Secrets never leave user's device
