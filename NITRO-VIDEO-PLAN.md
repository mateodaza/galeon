# Nitro Application Video (2 minutes)

Target: Nitro reviewers scanning hundreds of apps for 15 spots.
Goal: Make them want to talk to you. Not a product demo. Not a pitch deck.
Tone: Two builders showing real work. Honest. No hype.

---

## Scene 1 — Faces (0:00–0:20)

**Format:** Both founders on camera. Natural setting, not a staged background.

**Script (adapt, don't read):**
"I'm Mateo, 7 years building Web3 products. Led engineering at Giveth, led frontend at Asymmetry Finance. This is Carlos — we've been building together since Giveth. We just won the Mantle Hackathon RWA track. This is Galeon — privacy infrastructure for on-chain finance."

**Notes:**

- Look at camera, not a script
- No slides, no logos behind you
- Phone camera is fine, just make sure lighting is decent

---

## Scene 2 — The Problem (0:20–0:35)

**Format:** Still on camera.

**Script (adapt):**
"Every on-chain transaction is public. Payroll, vendor payments, treasury moves — all visible to anyone with a block explorer. Businesses can't operate like this. The only privacy tools that existed, like Tornado Cash, got sanctioned because they had zero compliance. You had to choose: privacy or compliance. We built the thing that does both."

**Notes:**

- 15 seconds max. Don't over-explain.
- The Tornado Cash reference lands because Haseeb (Dragonfly) is a Nitro mentor and lived this.

---

## Scene 3 — The Product (0:35–1:15)

**Format:** Screen recording of the real app on Mantle mainnet. Mateo narrates over the recording.

**Show these four things, in order:**

1. **Create a Port** — Click, stealth address appears.
   - Narrate: "This is a Port. One click, the receiver gets a unique stealth address. No one can link payments to the same person."

2. **Pay through the link** — Open the payment link, send a transaction.
   - Narrate: "A payer opens this link and sends funds. A fresh stealth address is generated just for this payment."

3. **Pool withdrawal via relayer** — Show the withdrawal. Point out that the receiver's address doesn't appear in the tx.
   - Narrate: "The receiver deposits into our privacy pool and withdraws through a relayer. Their address never shows up on-chain. The ZK proof verifies the funds are clean without revealing which deposit is theirs."

4. **Shipwreck tax report** — Generate the PDF. Show it briefly.
   - Narrate: "And here's the compliance layer. Shipwreck generates a tax report on demand. US and Colombian jurisdictions. Privacy and compliance in one flow."

**Close the screen recording:**
"This entire flow works on mainnet today. Not a testnet. Not a mockup."

**Notes:**

- Pre-record the screen capture so it's smooth. Don't fumble live.
- Speed up any loading/waiting in editing. Every second counts.
- Keep the browser clean — no other tabs, no bookmark bar clutter.
- If a step takes more than 10 seconds on screen, cut it.

---

## Scene 4 — Why It's Different (1:15–1:35)

**Format:** Back on camera.

**Script (adapt):**
"Galeon combines three things nobody else has put together in one protocol. Stealth addresses from EIP-5564 for receiver privacy. Privacy Pools — the model Vitalik co-authored — for sender privacy. And Shipwreck for tax compliance. The combination doesn't exist anywhere else. We're building on standards the Ethereum ecosystem just finalized, at the exact moment Vitalik and the Ethereum Foundation are making privacy a first-class priority."

**Notes:**

- Don't say "Vitalik supports us." Say the standards and research exist, you're building on them.
- 20 seconds. Don't linger.

---

## Scene 5 — The Ask (1:35–2:00)

**Format:** On camera. Both founders visible if possible.

**Script (adapt):**
"We have zero users. We're honest about that. We built the protocol first because with ZK proofs, it either works or it doesn't — there's no faking it. Now we need help turning a working protocol into a used product. Nitro's product teardowns, growth experiments, and Demo Day in front of investors who understand infrastructure bets — that's exactly what gets us from here to first integrators. Privacy infrastructure is a decade-long build. We're committed to it."

**Notes:**

- End looking at camera. Confident, not desperate.
- Don't apologize for zero users. State it, explain why, move forward.
- The "decade-long build" line matches Nitro's "5-10 year founders" criteria.
