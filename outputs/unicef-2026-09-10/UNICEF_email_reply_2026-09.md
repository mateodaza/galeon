Dear Livia and team,

Thank you for the clarification. We understand that the priority is Galeon's privacy technology and evidence that people can use it. Q1 covers testnet integration and feasibility; Q2 an integrated candidate and initial independent review; Q3 review closure and capped real-USDC use; and Q4 sustained activity and independent deployment. To address your specific points:

1. **ASP control.** The prototype model gives Cartagena On Chain ASP control. Q2 delivers self-hosting and handover tooling, including manual correction and tested exits. Q3 demonstrates operator replacement with superseded powers revoked. Self-hosting and handover are deliverables, not today's operating model.

2. **Selective disclosure.** Tax-report PDFs do not provide this proof. Q1 demonstrates feasibility; Q2 delivers consent-based export and a runnable verifier for supported funding histories. By Q3, at least five provenance packages will be checked externally. Off-ramps retain their own acceptance decisions.

3. **Governance and relayers.** Q2 implements timelocked privileged changes and self-hostable relayer configuration, reviewed before external use. Q3 demonstrates alternate-relayer failover. Fabio will seek an independent operator; if that target is not achieved, we will report it separately rather than call team-operated failover decentralisation.

4. **Migration and portability.** Base/native USDC is preferred, subject to a day-30 compatibility decision; ERC-20 support does not eliminate full-stack integration. Confidence is medium for Q1 integration and medium-low for release timing. As of 8 September 2026, the hosted backend was paused; the earlier 81 deposits / 14 users are historical figures. The Mantle prototype includes a development-setup circuit; the reviewed release requires public source, setup provenance and review of our changes. A second-EVM test deployment runs across Q3–Q4, without a second production pool.

5. **Mobile usability.** Q1 establishes physical-device baselines. Before external use in Q3, we target warm-cache proof generation at p95 ≤60 seconds and ≥95% completion on two low-end Android models. Wallet handoff and interrupted-transaction recovery are release conditions.

6. **Privacy floor.** We have not established a defensible numerical guarantee. Q1 defines the threat model and assessment method; Q2 delivers an independently reviewed operating-floor policy and visible warnings before sensitive use. Would you accept this dated deliverable instead of an unsupported number now?

This remains a separate liquidity objective, not something inflows alone prove; it requires external resources that are not yet secured. Team-funded activity is excluded; these measures do not guarantee anonymity.
