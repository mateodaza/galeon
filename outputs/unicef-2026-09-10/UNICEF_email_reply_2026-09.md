# Subject: Galeon — revised one-year workplan and USD 70,000 proposal

Dear Livia and team,

Thank you for the further feedback. We have revised the proposal to **USD 70,000** and would prefer funding in **USDC**. This workplan replaces August's version. It focuses on Galeon's open-source privacy protocol—stealth addresses, the pool and compliance tools—with one mobile-first web application (PWA), not a broader aid-delivery platform.

Q1 covers testnet integration and feasibility; Q2 brings the components together for initial independent review. Q3 closes review findings and starts limited real-USDC use; Q4 tests sustained use and independent deployment. Launch is planned by mid-month 9, allowing at least two weeks of observed use in Q3. Development continues throughout the year.

To address your specific points:

1. **ASP control.** Cartagena On Chain controls the prototype ASP. We propose it as the initial ASP operator for the limited trial, subject to Q1 legal/role assessment and release checks. Q2 adds documented acceptance, rejection and correction decisions that survive rebuilds, plus self-hosting and handover. Tested exits cover eligible original depositors with the required proof, not every beneficiary. Q3 demonstrates replacement with old powers revoked.

2. **Selective disclosure.** The proof will link selected funding receipts to a completed withdrawal, including recipient, asset and amount, without sharing spending keys. Unsupported histories will be rejected. Q1 tests feasibility; Q2 delivers user-approved export and a runnable verifier; Q3 has at least five externally checked packages. Tax PDFs are not this proof, and off-ramps decide whether to accept it.

3. **Governance and relayers.** Q2 adds enforced delays for privileged changes and a self-hostable relayer, reviewed before external use. Q3 tests switching to an alternate relayer. Fabio will recruit an independent relayer operator; if unsuccessful, we will report that target missed. Team-run failover does not demonstrate independence.

4. **Migration and portability.** Base/native USDC remains preferred, with compatibility decided by day 30. Our contracts already support ERC-20; integration across the application is still needed. Confidence is medium for Q1 integration and medium-low for release timing. As of 8 September 2026, the hosted backend was paused; 81 deposits / 14 users are historical. The Mantle prototype includes a development-setup circuit; release requires public source, verified setup records, no development-key artifacts and review of our changes. A second-EVM test starts in Q3 and completes in Q4, without another production pool.

5. **Mobile usability.** Before external use, two low-end Android models must meet a 95th-percentile proof-generation time of ≤60 seconds with files cached, and ≥95% proof-generation completion. They must also pass Q1's first-use/network thresholds, wallet handoff and recovery checks. Q4 repeats these tests.

6. **Privacy floor.** We have not established a defensible numerical guarantee. Q1 defines the threat model and assessment method; Q2 delivers an independently reviewed operating-floor policy and visible warnings before sensitive use. Please confirm whether this proposed Q1 assessment and Q2 policy meet your request.

We propose 10 independent participants / USD 2,500 in new external inflows in Q3, rising to 40 / USD 10,000 cumulatively in Q4. Q3 reports available daily balances; Q4 separately targets a USD 5,000 median balance over its final 30 days. This needs external funds that are not yet secured, with withdrawals and replenishment assessed separately. Grant/team-funded principal is excluded. Inflows do not establish retained balances or anonymity.

The four letters already shared—from Fundación Más Music, ASOASAR, Fundación Innovación Próspera and Corporación Sinergia—support partner development. Más Music remains an anchor candidate. In Q1, Fabio will qualify staff, participants and funding sources, separating commitments from pending resources. A beneficiary pilot remains partner-operated and requires resources and readiness checks; a staff rehearsal is not a substitute. UNICEF is not assumed to deploy Galeon, and we do not undertake fiat delivery.

Two full-time engineers and one full-time operations lead cover the twelve months, with named responsibilities and time allocations per milestone. We will test fixed-fee implementation and support offers with institutional buyers and use their responses to refine the post-grant maintenance plan.

Could you also confirm whether the testnet demonstration meets your portability expectation, whether general-administration costs are eligible, and the anticipated start date and first-tranche timing?
